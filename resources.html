Apps, workbooks, helplines, directories and communities I'd point a friend towards, sorted by topic.

Last updated November 2026

Most of these are free. A lot of them were first gathered by the volunteers at FMHY, and I've added others I trust. I'm not connected to any of them. If a site asks you for money or personal details and something feels off, close it.

If you're looking for advice rather than links, the guide covers anxiety, depression, sleep, grief, getting therapy and more.

In crisis?

Call your local emergency number, or see the full list of crisis lines by country. In the US, call or text 988. In the UK and Ireland, call Samaritans on 116 123.
